Things We Carry

Stories, poems, and quiet truths for the hearts still learning how to say what they feel.

Read / Download Authoritative PDF
Prologue

A Note Before the First Page

Some things are carried quietly.
A memory. A question. A person. A dream. A life that did not turn out exactly as expected.
Poem Parlor was made for those things—the feelings that do not always know how to introduce themselves, but somehow recognize us when we read them.
This is Collection I.
A room for the things we carry.
